About Me
What I build. I engineer production-grade systems end to end, including microservices and backend platforms (gRPC, NestJS, Docker), cloud native systems, full-stack applications, and applied ML/LLM pipelines, with an emphasis on clean architecture, scalability, and deployability.

What I research.
